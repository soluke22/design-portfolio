Projects

When The Sun Sets is a Vampire Survivors-style game where players manage a bed & breakfast during the day and defend it from cryptid invasions at night. I led systems design, developing day/night cycles, enemy AI behaviors, and economy tuning. My work includes stat balancing, roguelike loop logic, and dynamic NPC roles across shifting survival phases.

Dive In

Merlin is an action RPG where players choose melee, ranged, or magic pathways. I led this project and focused on combat and level design, shifting the levels to develop based on the aspects of combat the player could choose.

Stardust is a sci-fi RPG framework built from the ground up to test narrative systems and balance via tabletop prototyping. I created modular stat formulas, character classes, and a branching story. This design served as a pre-production blueprint for a full digital RPG.
